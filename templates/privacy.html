Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data Spendly collects, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We keep things simple — we collect only what is necessary to run the service.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and hashed password when you register
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually
  • Usage data — pages visited and actions taken within the app, used only to fix bugs and improve the experience

We do not collect payment information, bank account details, or any financial data from external sources. Everything in Spendly is entered by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To authenticate you and display your expense records
  • To generate summaries and breakdowns within your account
  • To send essential service emails (e.g., password resets)
  • To diagnose errors and improve application performance

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the service. We do not sell your data to anyone.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database accessible only to your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, all associated records will be permanently removed from our systems. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond 30 days.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is completely immune to security risks. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces used in the interface; Google may log your IP address as part of this request

We do not integrate with analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media trackers. No third party has access to your expense data.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you may:

  • View all expense records stored in your account
  • Edit or delete any individual record
  • Request a full export of your data by contacting us
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of Spendly after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about your privacy or data? Contact us at hello@spendly.app.
